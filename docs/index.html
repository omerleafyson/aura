AURA Snap — Huawei AppGallery Privacy and Data Rights Notice

Updated: 12 November 2026

Scope and operator This notice applies to the AURA Snap Huawei AppGallery edition, version 8.0.15 (1080), package com.auratechnology.aurasnap. It describes this edition, which works without account registration and does not offer advertising, cloud library synchronisation or in-app purchases. Other editions, including Google Play, can have different advertising and purchase features.

The developer account provider is Emine Yapraklioglu. The product is presented under the AURA TECHNOLOGY / AURA Snap brand. For privacy, access, correction or deletion requests contact privacy@aurasnap.tr; for product support contact support@aurasnap.tr. Do not send passwords, payment-card information or identity documents in an initial request.

Information used - No account registration: this edition does not ask for an email address, password or Google account, and does not create a Firebase guest account. - Recognition: the photos, screenshots, representative video frames, audio recordings, dialogue, search terms or URLs you choose to submit, together with extracted text, visual signals and recognition results. - Saved activity: recognition history, favourites, collections and discovery preferences or interactions that you choose to save are held locally on the device. This edition does not synchronise a library to a cloud account. - Technical information: connection information, request timing, application version and error/security records processed by the app and its infrastructure providers.

The Huawei edition does not request contacts, SMS, call logs or precise GPS location. The edition does not load advertising or initiate Google Play purchases. Camera, microphone, notifications and screen-capture access are used only for relevant features and subject to Android's permission/confirmation controls. Choosing an item to upload does not mean all device media are uploaded.

Purposes and processing Information is used to respond to recognition/search requests, provide movie and TV metadata, save chosen records locally, answer support and privacy requests, and operate and protect the service. Providing recognition media or saving records locally is your choice; without an input the corresponding recognition request cannot be performed.

Depending on the activity and applicable law, processing may be needed to provide the service you request, comply with legal obligations, maintain service security, or rely on permission/consent where required. An Android permission is not consent to unrelated uses.

Providers and international processing This Huawei edition uses Supabase for API and backend processing, TMDB for movie/TV metadata, and configured recognition/AI services for requested analyses. Firebase account creation, authentication and cloud library synchronisation are disabled in this edition. Processing can take place outside your country. The connected AURA Snap Supabase project is hosted in the Japan region; provider services can use additional locations.

Requested recognition input may be sent to the app's backend and its configured recognition/AI processors. AURA Snap does not claim that all processing takes place solely on the device. AI output can contain errors and is not a guarantee of a correct match. The Huawei app labels generated scene analysis as AI-generated.

Retention and deletion The app uses temporary local files to prepare media requests and attempts to delete those temporary files after processing. Saved history and collections can remain on your device until you remove them. Backend/provider operational, security, backup and legally required records may have different retention periods. This notice does not promise immediate deletion of every copy held by every infrastructure provider.

Use the in-app history controls to request removal of saved history. The Huawei edition creates no remote user account. Use Android Settings > Apps > AURA Snap > Storage > Clear data to remove its local library and settings, or uninstall the app. For requests relating to submitted media or server/provider logs, contact the privacy address; clearing local app storage does not itself erase provider logs.

For information about a particular stored record, the applicable retention period or a deletion request, contact privacy@aurasnap.tr. We may request proportionate information about the request or processing involved before acting. This edition has no account password to disclose.

Your rights Depending on the law that applies to you, you may request information about processing, access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a portable copy, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may contact the relevant data-protection authority. Send requests to privacy@aurasnap.tr and describe the relevant processing and the action requested. The support address is an additional contact if a request cannot be delivered.

Security and children Network requests use HTTPS. This edition does not give access to authenticated accounts or purchase entitlements. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. This is a film/TV discovery app; catalogue descriptions, artwork and trailers may relate to mature-rated productions. A movie's availability in the catalogue is not a recommendation that it is suitable for children. The store's final age rating and applicable parental/consent rules must be observed.

Updates The date above identifies this notice. Changes to the Huawei edition's data use require corresponding updates to the notice and store declarations.
